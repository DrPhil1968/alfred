Alfred — 3D look test

Stand-in character only. This page tests the style, the lip-sync and the delay, not the final Alfred.

Press Start. Alfred will ask six sample questions and listen for your answer.

Characters: sample models from the TalkingHead project, for non-commercial testing only — “Man — realistic” made with Avatar SDK, “Woman — realistic” made with Avaturn, “Woman — game style” made with Ready Player Me (CC BY-NC 4.0). 3D engine: TalkingHead by met4citizen (MIT). Voice and listening use your browser’s built-in speech, so nothing is sent to a paid service.
